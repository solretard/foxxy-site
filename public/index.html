FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Kraftera live?+

Yes. Create an account, link your wallet, and start buying and selling today.

Which wallets can I use?+

MetaMask, Phantom, and any mobile wallet that supports WalletConnect. You sign a free message to link it. Nothing is charged and no funds move.

Which chains can I get paid on?+

Ethereum, Base, Solana and XRPL. You pick the chain when you list an item.

What can I sell?+

Digital art, music and sample packs, ebooks, templates (website, Figma, Notion, decks) and NFTs.

How much does it cost?+

Listing is free. A small fee is taken only when something sells.

Do I need crypto to sign up?+

No. Sign up with your email first. You'll need a wallet to buy or to get paid.
