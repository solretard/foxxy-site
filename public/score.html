How your score is built

01

NFT Holdings

1 pt per NFT held from any collection, up to 40 pts. Any active NFT wallet earns points here.

02

Collection Breadth

3 pts per unique collection held (up to 15 collections) — collectors who range widely score higher.

03

Mint Activity

Points for every verified on-chain mint — being early and minting directly always earns more.

04

Blue-Chip Holdings

Bonus pts for holding FOXXY's curated blue-chips: BAYC, CryptoPunks, Azuki, Pudgy Penguins and more.

05

Long Hold Bonus

+10 pts per curated project held for 30+ days — diamond hands over flippers, always.

06

Featured Boost

Activity in featured projects (currently Azuki) earns a 1.5× multiplier across holdings, mints, and more.
