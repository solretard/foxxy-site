How your score is built

Your wallet earns points across six categories. Highest-ranked wallets get guaranteed mint spots. Everyone else is ranked into tiers.

01

Curated project holdings

Hold NFTs from verified Ethereum projects. Rarer pieces score higher — floor to top 1%.

Floor = 10 pts · Top 10% = 25 pts · Top 1% = 50 pts
02

Trading volume

Cumulative ETH traded across buys and sells. Marketplace-verified only — wash trades excluded.

1 pt per 0.1 ETH of verified volume
03

Holding duration

No points under the minimum hold window. Full points only after you've crossed the threshold.

Gate: 14–30 days minimum hold
04

Collection diversity

Holding across multiple curated projects earns a flat bonus per additional project.

+5 pts per project beyond the first · capped
05

Mint activity

Minting directly from project contracts earns micro-points, capped per project to stop farming.

2 pts per verified mint · capped per project
06

Featured project boost

A rotating project is added to the curated list periodically, carrying a time-limited multiplier.

1.5× on activity tied to featured project

Built to stay honest

A points system is only as good as its ability to resist manipulation. Three layers keep it clean.

Wallet verification

Sign-In with Ethereum ties your score to your actual wallet. Nobody can claim a whale wallet that isn't theirs.

Wash-trade filtering

Sales flagged by Reservoir and OpenSea as wash trades are excluded. Same-cluster buy/sell loops don't count toward volume.

Sybil detection

Wallets sharing funding sources or transaction patterns are clustered and capped — one person can't run ten wallets for ten spots.

How spots are awarded

The leaderboard is public. Paste your address and see exactly where you stand and what it would take to move up.
